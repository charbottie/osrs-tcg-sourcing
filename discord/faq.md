    # FAQ
    
    ## Will I lose all my credits and cards after the beta is done?
    
    The planned force wipe of beta collections has been cancelled. When the full release update ships, there will be a clear distinction between beta collections and collections started after the update. Existing beta collections will keep their cards and credits and will be able to still open packs, but will no longer be able to trade with other players unless the collection is manually reset using the sidebar panel after the update. A date for this change has not been specified yet.

    ## Current Credit Rates (⁠⁠general⁠)
    
    - 100 credits / 1000 xp will probably be left untouched apart from tweaking some combat skill related stuff
    - Combat level equivalent amount of credits for killing things will probably stay as it is with some exceptions
    - Combat related exceptions such as cox, where monsters do not have combat levels or multi-phase boss encounters that would not naturally give kill credit.
    - Level-up bonuses a system that gives scaling amounts of credits for leveling up to give early game players credits for packs as XP rates can be very underwhelming

5. ## Dink and Webhook? Which one do I use?
    
    If you utilize Dink you do not need to fill in the Webhook URL in OSRS TCG's settings - just check the Dink box instead
    
6. ## Thousands Separator
    
    The plugin utilizes a space for the thousands separator. Since some areas of the world use comma, and some use periods, it was decided to use a space instead. So 2 500 is two-thousand five hundred. This will be shown all throughout the plugin
    
7. ## Why can't I trade?
    
    You need to have the Party plugin enabled. You can also use the Hub Party Panel plugin, but the base Party plugin **needs** to also be enabled.
    
8. ## Will more packs be introduced?
    
    Yes. In the full version more packs will be introduced.
    
9. ## Does my collection sync across computers?
    
    You can sync your collections and progress via Runelite's profile sync. Make sure you log out / close the client on the first device before opening the client on the second one to make sure it syncs before starting over on your next device

11. ## What is the credit curve like?
    
    Early game credit gains very heavily rely on credits from level-ups, which should become more insignificant as you unlock better training methods at higher levels. At any stage of the game you should be able to reach decent amount of packs if that's what you focus on.
    
12. ## Are all cards able to be foil?
    
    Any card has a chance to be a foil, just a rare version of the card with increased score. Collection score uses foil card scores before normal edition cards. They only matter for collection score and collection foil percentages.
