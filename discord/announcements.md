    #  ![:bronzeman:](https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1528227649409908776.webp?size=96) Bronzeman TCG Update ![:bronzeman:](https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1528227649409908776.webp?size=96) Update is live! ![:wuh:](https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1504925486713671850.webp?size=96) 
    
    ## Settings update and Locked Item Indicators! The config panel got a small rework:
    
    - NPC Locks: one dropdown (Off / Prevent Combat / Prevent Interaction / Hide NPCs) replaces the old combat/spell/hide toggles. Prevent Combat blocks combat but keeps NPCs talkable.
    - Quest NPCs are now smart: NPCs from quests you've started (or finished) always stay visible and talkable, even on the strictest settings — no more quest-bricking, no toggle needed. Attack should still require you to have the card.
    - Item settings: Ground Items, Item Usage, Banking, Grand Exchange and Coin Settings dropdowns. Heads up — defaults are stricter now: locked items show only Drop/Destroy (no card, no permit!), banking locked items is deposit-only, and shops always refuse locked purchases. Use the exempt list or the dropdowns if you want it softer.
    
    - Locked Item Indicators: Turn these on to change your inventory and bank items to react based on Locked status.
    
    Fade: Icon will go semi transparent to indicate it's still locked. Fade+Icon: Same as Fade but will show a small ![🚫](https://discord.com/assets/6c4edbf76bb07c39.svg) on the item also. This is in "Visuals" on the settings.
    
    - Thieving tiers: Off / Coins+Pouch / +NPC card / All-loot, with H.A.M. and Master Farmer Insanity toggles for the brave.
    
    Thieving has had a bit more of a refined update and testing pass. Other skills are currently underway! Your deliberate setting choices carry over automatically; anything you'd turned off stays off.
